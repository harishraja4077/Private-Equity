Our Story

From a Two-Person Advisory to a Global PE Platform

Stackly began in 2008 inauspiciously, amid the world's deepest financial crisis with a simple thesis: fundamental diligence and patient capital can outperform fear. What started as a two-person advisory now operates out of Stackly MMR Complex, Chinna Thirupathi, near Chinna Muniyappan Kovil, Salem, Tamil Nadu 636008.

Today we manage $4.2B across 38 portfolio companies with a team of 60+ investment, operating and legal professionals yet our process remains deliberately boutique: every deal is staffed end-to-end by partners.

  • 44 team members 6 sector verticals 3 continents
  • AUM grown 12 since 2015
  • 98% of founders would invest with us again
